Up and running in three steps

Install once. After that it's just select → Sync.

Install the AE panel

Download the release, run sh install.sh, restart After Effects and open Window ▸ Extensions ▸ Figma2AE.

Add the Figma plugin

In the Figma desktop app: Plugins ▸ Development ▸ Import plugin from manifest… and pick manifest.json.

Select & Sync

Select frames or layers, choose Break Apart on or off, press Sync Selection. Done.

Break Apart, or keep it grouped

The toggle at the top of the plugin decides how your design is laid out in After Effects.

Break Apart: ON

Best for animating individual elements.

  • Every Figma layer becomes its own AE layer
  • Frames & groups become parents (nulls or background layers)
  • Masks become track mattes, clipped frames become precomps

Break Apart: OFF

Best for keeping your timeline clean.

  • Each selection becomes one shape layer
  • The Figma hierarchy is kept as nested shape groups
  • Only text, images, gradients and effects get their own layers
